LayanKita UAT: 55 scenarios, all passed after two rounds

The user acceptance test plan and results: 55 scenarios across the mobile app, the web dashboard and end-to-end flows, run face to face with the client in two rounds 10 days apart.

My role
Wrote the scenarios and ran UAT with QA
Scope
35 mobile, 15 web, 5 end-to-end
Context
Internship project. App, company, client, places and people are anonymized
Date
Internship Feb 2025 to Feb 2026. Rewritten for portfolio Nov 2026
Tools
Excel
Deliverables
UAT workbook with findings and sign-off

The question

Does the app do what the SRS promised, on the kind of phone residents actually use?

Background

UAT was run on a staging server with a test APK on a low-end Android phone, with the client watching. Each scenario points back to an SRS requirement.

Method

  1. Wrote every scenario from an SRS requirement, with steps, the expected result and the requirement reference.
  2. Ran round 1 with the client and QA, and logged pass or fail for each scenario.
  3. Recorded 5 findings with severity, follow-up and status.
  4. Retested the failed scenarios in round 2, 10 days later.
  5. Summarized the results and prepared the sign-off sheet.

What is inside

  • 52 of 55 passed in round 1. Three mobile scenarios failed: slow pages, a job listing page that would not open, and staff attendance login.
  • 55 of 55 passed after round 2. All three were fixed and retested.
  • 5 findings. Three fixed, two low severity monitored after release.
  • Decision: accepted with notes. No high severity findings left open.
