LayanKita SRS, mobile app: 86 functional requirements

The specification for the Android app residents use, from sign up and complaints to QRIS ticket payment, location-checked QR tickets and staff attendance.

My role
System Analyst intern, author
Scope
Android app for residents and staff
Context
Internship project. App, company, client, places and people are anonymized
Date
Internship Feb 2025 to Feb 2026. Rewritten for portfolio Nov 2026
Method
MoSCoW priority per requirement
Deliverables
23-page SRS

The question

What must the Android app do on a mid-range phone with an unstable signal?

Background

Most residents use mid-range Android phones and the signal is not always stable. The app has no offline mode and asks permission before using location, camera, gallery, notifications or fingerprint.

Method

  1. Collected inputs from the BRD, the PRD, the web SRS, anonymized screenshots, UAT notes and the benchmark.
  2. Wrote 86 functional requirements with priority and agreement status.
  3. Set the operating environment and assumptions, such as GPS accurate enough to check distance up to 300 meters.
  4. Recorded where the developer's manual differs from the app, so the gap is visible before release.

What is inside

  • 86 functional requirements. Sign up, complaints, tourism, tickets, health consultation, MPP reservation, local shops and attendance.
  • Anti-scalper tickets. A tour ticket can only be shown at the destination and scanned once.
  • QRIS payment. Every ticket and venue booking is paid and recorded digitally.
  • Known gaps. Differences between the manual and the app are listed in one place.
