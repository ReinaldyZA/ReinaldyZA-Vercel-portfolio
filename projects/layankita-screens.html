LayanKita screens: the mobile app and the web dashboard

Anonymized screenshots of the Android app and the admin dashboard, plus mockups for screens that had no screenshot yet.

My role
System Analyst intern, documented the screens
Scope
Mobile app and web dashboard
Context
Internship project. App, company, client, places and people are anonymized
Date
Internship Feb 2025 to Feb 2026. Rewritten for portfolio Nov 2026
Tools
Screenshots and mockups
Deliverables
67 screens

The question

What do residents and admins actually see?

Background

Screens make the requirements concrete. Real screenshots were anonymized: names changed, faces and personal data blurred. Mockups fill the gaps for flows that had no screenshot yet, such as complaints and QR tickets.

Method

  1. Captured each mobile and web screen and removed or blurred identifying details.
  2. Used mockups for flows without a screenshot yet.
  3. Linked the screens to the PRD stories and the SRS requirements.

What is inside

  • 26 mobile screenshots. Login, home, call center, AI assistant, news, health consultation, MPP reservation and more.
  • 10 mobile mockups. Create a complaint, complaint status, rate the handling, tour and event tickets, QR ticket and bookings.
  • 31 web screenshots. Eight of them are shown here, from the overview to complaints and data sync.
