Cookaid PRD: 14 user stories with acceptance criteria

The product requirement document for Cookaid, a recipe app concept. It defines the problem, success metrics, stakeholders, user stories, milestones and release phases in one place.

My role
Wrote the PRD (solo)
Scope
9 features, 14 user stories
Program
MySkill Product & Project Management, and Figma for UI/UX
Date
October 2024
Tools
Google Docs, Figma, Jira
Deliverables
11-page PRD

The question

What exactly should Cookaid do, and how will the team know each feature is done?

Background

Home cooks look for ideas on social media, watch tutorials on video platforms and save recipes in notes. Cookaid is a concept app that puts discovery, learning and sharing in one place. The PRD is the document developers, designers and QA would work from.

Method

  1. Listed four success metrics, each with a target, such as 10,000 monthly active users three months after launch.
  2. Mapped stakeholders with RACI: who decides, who is accountable, who builds, who is consulted and who is informed.
  3. Wrote 14 user stories, each with a user journey, a story and testable acceptance criteria, and attached the matching Figma screen.
  4. Set nine milestones and a three-phase release, from initial launch to full rollout.

What is inside

  • 14 user stories. Each one maps to a journey, acceptance criteria and a Figma screen.
  • Testable criteria. For example: password at least 10 characters with one number, and a clear error for a wrong email.
  • Measurable goals. DAU/MAU of at least 30%, 2,000 recipes shared and 50,000 tutorial views in the first three months.
  • A staged rollout. Core flow first, social and video features second, optimization third.
