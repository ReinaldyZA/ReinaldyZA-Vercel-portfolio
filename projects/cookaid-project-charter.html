Cookaid project charter: scope, team and success criteria on one page

The one-page agreement that starts the project: what Cookaid will and will not build, who is involved, how success is measured and what could go wrong.

My role
Project manager (solo, course project)

9 features, mobile app only
Program

October 2024
Tools

Deliverables
1-page project charter

The question

Before anyone builds anything, what are we agreeing to deliver, by when, and how will we judge it?

Background

A charter keeps the sponsor, the project manager and the team on the same page. For Cookaid it sets the 6 October 2024 to 1 March 2025 window that the WBS, Gantt chart and sprint backlog all follow.

Method

  1. Defined nine features in scope and two out of scope: payments and a web version.
  2. Listed the resources: development, UX, QA and marketing.
  3. Turned the goals into four measurable success criteria.
  4. Named three risks with a mitigation for each, such as seeding 200+ recipes before release so the app does not launch empty.

What is inside

  • 9 features in scope. From splash screen and sign in to video tutorials and saved recipes.
  • Clear limits. Payments and a web version are out of scope for phase 1.
  • 4 success criteria. Each with a number and a time frame.
  • 3 risks. API delays, empty content at launch and low UAT participation, each with a mitigation.
