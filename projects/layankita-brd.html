LayanKita BRD: 24 business requirements for a public service app

The business requirements document for LayanKita, a regency government's public service app with an Android app for residents and a web dashboard for admins.

My role
System Analyst intern, author
Scope
Android app and web dashboard
Context
Internship project. App, company, client, places and people are anonymized
Date
Internship Feb 2025 to Feb 2026. Rewritten for portfolio Nov 2026
Sources
Observation, client interviews, review analysis, benchmark
Deliverables
13-page BRD, public version

The question

Why rebuild the old app, and what must the new one achieve for residents and the government?

Background

Residents reported problems through WhatsApp, phone calls or in person, and each report was logged somewhere different. Destination tickets were sold only at the counter and resold by scalpers. The old app collected 1-star reviews because ticket orders failed when the server was full, so the government rebuilt it as LayanKita.

Method

  1. Observed the mobile app and the admin dashboard, and logged findings such as test data mixed in with real reports.
  2. Ran a sentiment analysis of the old app's Google Play reviews.
  3. Benchmarked two regional public service apps, JAKI and Sadayana, to set a minimum standard and pick differentiators.
  4. Mapped the stakeholders and seven user roles, then drew the as-is and to-be complaint process.
  5. Wrote 8 business objectives, 24 business requirements and 13 non-functional requirements, with assumptions, risks and phases.

What is inside

  • 7 problems. From reports nobody can track to an app that fails under ticket load.
  • 8 business objectives. Written as service rules, for example no report left as New for more than 2 working days.
  • 24 business requirements. Complaints, emergency numbers, tourism, events, sports, public information and staff attendance.
  • As-is and to-be. One complaint queue with eight statuses, from New to Closed.
