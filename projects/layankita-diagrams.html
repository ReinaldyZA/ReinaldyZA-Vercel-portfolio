22 diagrams: 10 UML, 10 ERD, 2 flowcharts

The question

How do the processes, screens and data fit together, in a form developers can build from?

Background

Each diagram answers one question. Flowcharts show the business process, use cases show who does what, activity and sequence diagrams show a single flow, state machines show how a status changes, and ERDs show what is stored.

Method

  1. Drew the complaint process as it is today and as it should be.
  2. Drew one use case diagram for the web dashboard and one for the mobile app.
  3. Picked the riskiest flows for activity and sequence diagrams: complaints, ticket purchase, QRIS payment and QR ticket scanning.
  4. Modeled the complaint and ticket statuses as state machines.
  5. Split the database into nine module ERDs plus one combined transaction ERD.

What is inside

  • 2 flowcharts. Complaint process, as-is and to-be.
  • 2 use case diagrams. Web dashboard and mobile app.
  • 4 activity and 2 sequence diagrams. Complaints, tour tickets, QRIS payment and QR scanning.
  • 2 state machines and 10 ERDs. Complaint and ticket status, and the data per module.
