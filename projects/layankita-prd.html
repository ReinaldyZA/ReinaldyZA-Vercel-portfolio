LayanKita PRD: 34 user stories with acceptance criteria

The product requirement document turns the BRD into 34 user stories for the mobile app and the web dashboard. Each story has a user journey, acceptance criteria and its screen.

My role
System Analyst intern, author
Scope
34 user stories, mobile and web
Context
Internship project. App, company, client, places and people are anonymized
Date
Internship Feb 2025 to Feb 2026. Rewritten for portfolio Nov 2026
Reviewer
Supervising System Analyst
Deliverables
22-page PRD

The question

What should each feature do, and how will the client know it is done?

Background

The PRD follows the same template I used for the Cookaid PRD. Complaints come first because they were the problem seen most often during observation.

Method

  1. Set five product metrics, such as the share of complaints sent through the app and a median first response under one working day.
  2. Mapped who decides, who is accountable, who builds, who is consulted and who is informed.
  3. Wrote 34 user stories grouped by feature, each with a user journey and acceptance criteria.
  4. Placed the real screenshot or a mockup next to every story.

What is inside

  • 34 user stories. From sign in and complaints to QRIS tickets, health consultation and staff attendance.
  • Testable criteria. For example: the sign in button stays disabled until both email and password are filled.
  • Screens next to stories. Reviewers see the requirement and the screen side by side.
  • 5 product metrics. Each one tied to a business objective in the BRD.
