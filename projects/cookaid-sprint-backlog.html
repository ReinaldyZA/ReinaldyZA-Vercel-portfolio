Cookaid sprint backlog: 96 story points across 9 sprints

The 14 user stories from the PRD, plus 14 supporting tasks, planned into nine two-week sprints with story points, owners, priorities and acceptance criteria.

The question

How do 14 user stories turn into a sprint plan a team can actually run?

Background

The backlog connects the PRD to the schedule. Every US-xx row is a user story from the PRD, and every T-xx row is a supporting task such as an API review or a UAT session.

Method

  1. Estimated each item in story points on the Fibonacci scale.
  2. Grouped build work into sprints 1 to 4, then testing and release into sprints 5 to 9.
  3. Gave every item an owner, a priority and acceptance criteria.
  4. Added a status dropdown and formulas that total story points per sprint and overall.

What is inside

  • 96 story points. Calculated by formula, so totals update when an estimate changes.
  • 9 sprints, no overlap. Two weeks each, from 15 October 2024 to 1 March 2025.
  • Heaviest sprint: 23 points. Sprint 4 carries most of the frontend stories.
  • Traceable. Every story links back to its number in the PRD.
